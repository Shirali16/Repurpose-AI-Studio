## 🌟 What's New & Answers to Key Questions

### 1. 🔊 Why was there no sound before & How is it solved?
- **Previously:** Standard HTML5 sample video clips (like *Tears of Steel*) were quiet or muted by browser autoplay security policies, and the sound didn't match the spoken tech script.
- **Now Solved with Built-in Real AI Voice:** We added **Web Speech Synthesis** right into the player! When you hit **"▶ Play Clip"**, your computer's speakers **actually speak the transcript aloud in real time** with natural voice and pace (*"Here is the brutal truth nobody in Silicon Valley wants to admit..."*).
- **Synchronized Karaoke Subtitles:** Every word glows and scales up in exact sync with the spoken voice!
- You can toggle voice on/off anytime with the **`🔊 Voice Audio: ON`** button.

### 2. 🏢 Nexus AI Media vs 🚀 GrowthLab Studio
| Dimension | Nexus AI Media | GrowthLab Studio |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Domain** | Deep Tech, AI Agents, Foundation Architecture | Product-Led Growth (PLG), SaaS Metrics, DTC |
| **Visual Identity** | Pastel Rose & Sky Blue with Sunny Gold highlights | Emerald Mint & Cyan with Amber highlights |
| **Anti-Patterns & Taboos** | Bans generic tech buzzwords (*delve, synergy, game-changer, revolutionize, paradigm shift*) | Bans get-rich-quick tropes (*growth hack, guru, passive income, skyrocket, 10x overnight*) |
| **Personas** | Executive B2B, Viral Tech Shorts, Community Insider | Product Growth Lead, DTC Performance |

### 3. 📁 Can I upload my own video or audio file?
**YES!** In the **"+ Ingest Media"** modal:
- You can now **drag and drop your own MP4, MP3, WAV, WebM, or MOV file** directly from your computer!
- The system automatically loads your local file into the player, analyzes it, and generates viral clips, hooks, and aspect framing immediately.

### 4. ⚡ What is the "Smart Offline Engine"?
Hackathon submissions are frequently judged **4 to 5 days later**. If external API keys expire, network quotas fail, or third-party servers go down, traditional AI tools crash. 
The **Smart Offline Engine** is our built-in heuristic NLP backup that guarantees:
- **Zero crashes**
- **Zero blank screens**
- **100% reliable evaluation even if totally offline**
- (You can still enter an optional Gemini API key in **Settings & Lab** if you want live API calls).

### 5. 🎨 Fun Pastel Palette & Clean Mobile Navigation
- **No more gloomy dark mode!** The app now defaults to **🌸 Pastel Pop** (warm cream background, soft pastel pinks, sky blues, sunshine butter yellows, and bubbly rounded cards).
- **Theme Switcher:** Choose between **🌸 Pastel Pop**, **🍦 Cotton Candy**, **☀️ Sunshine Glow**, or **🌙 Midnight Cyber** anytime in the top bar.
- **Uncluttered & Mobile-First:**

    - `📱 Studio` (Hero Player & Platform Copy)

  - On phones and mobile screens, it automatically shows a **touch-friendly Bottom Navigation Bar** like TikTok and CapCut!
